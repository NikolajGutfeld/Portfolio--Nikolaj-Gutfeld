About me

My name is Nikolaj Falck Gutfeld, and I am a 25-year-old IT Architecture student at Business Academy Copenhagen. I have a technical background and experience from several different industries, which has given me a practical, adaptable, and solution-oriented approach to both work and technology.

Before beginning my studies in IT Architecture, I studied as a passenger vehicle mechanic combined with a high school degree and completed my EUX qualification at Roskilde Technical School in 2023. My technical background has taught me how to work systematically, troubleshoot problems, understand complex systems, and find practical solutions.

Today, alongside my studies, I work part-time as an Assistant Export Coordinator, where my responsibilities include handling vehicles and documentation, coordinating payments, providing technical guidance, and communicating with international buyers.

Working in different industries and environments has taught me how to adapt quickly, communicate with different people, take responsibility, and approach challenges from both a technical and practical perspective. It has also given me an understanding of the importance of identifying what customers and business partners actually need, rather than simply focusing on the technical side of a task.

I am now building on these experiences through my studies in IT Architecture, where I am developing my knowledge of IT systems, software, infrastructure, and system design. The program also introduces the business side of developing IT solutions, including understanding customer and stakeholder needs and translating them into technical requirements, solutions, and products.

Outside of work and my studies, I have a strong interest in cars, technology, and understanding how things work. I enjoy working with people, taking on responsibility, and being in environments where no two days are exactly the same. I am naturally curious and enjoy learning new things, whether they are related to technology, business, or something completely different.

What motivates me most is being able to combine technical knowledge with practical problem-solving and working with people. This is also one of the reasons I chose to study IT Architecture, as it allows me to build on my technical background while developing new skills within IT and business.
